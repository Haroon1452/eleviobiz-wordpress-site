# Project Overview — ElevioBiz

## Business Background

ElevioBiz provides AI workflow automation services for healthcare organizations. Its offerings focus on patient management, receptionist automation, communication workflows, and administrative efficiency.

## Website Objectives

1. Present healthcare automation services clearly.
2. Explain solutions for different medical specialties.
3. Establish trust through company and founder information.
4. Educate potential customers through resources.
5. Encourage consultation and demonstration requests.

## Target Audience

- Dental clinics
- Dermatology practices
- Functional medicine providers
- Medical spas
- Plastic surgery practices
- Healthcare administrators

## Content Organization

The website uses service pages, specialty-focused content, educational resources, and contact opportunities to support prospective customers.

## Scope of Documentation

This project covers the marketing website, not the development or implementation of the company's AI automation products.
